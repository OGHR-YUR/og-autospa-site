Booking policy

The fine print, made simple

  • 50% deposit to book. The balance is due when the job's done.
  • Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours ahead with the link in your confirmation, and your deposit moves to the new date.
  • Under 24 hours' notice, a no-show, or a car we can't access means the deposit is forfeited.
  • Bad weather? Free reschedule.
  • We text you a reminder before your appointment.
Before we arrive

What we need

  • A parking spot for our van next to your car.
  • Access to a water spigot and a power outlet helps. If you don't have them, mention it when you book.
  • Valuables and personal items out of the car.
