FAQ

Good questions

How does booking work?

Pick your service, vehicle size and time on our booking page. A 50% deposit holds your spot, and the balance is due when the job's done.

Is sales tax included in your prices?

No. Prices are listed before tax, and Salt Lake County sales tax of 7.45% is added at checkout. Our price list shows the with-tax total too.

What do I need to have ready?
  • A parking spot where our van can pull up next to your car.
  • Access to a water spigot and a power outlet helps. If you don't have them, mention it when you book.
  • Valuables and personal items out of the car.
What are your hours?

We work Wednesday through Saturday. Wednesday appointments start from noon until midnight, and Thursday to Saturday from 8 AM until midnight. We're closed Sunday to Tuesday.

Do you charge extra for late appointments?

Yes. Jobs that start after 8 PM have a $50 after-hours fee added on top of the package price.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours ahead using the link in your confirmation, and your deposit moves to the new date. With less than 24 hours' notice, a no-show, or a car we can't access, the deposit is forfeited.

What if the weather is bad?

If weather gets in the way, we'll reschedule you for free.

Which package should I pick?

The Full Detail is our core job. Pick Deep Interior for stains, odors and kid or pet messes. The Maintenance Detail is only for cars professionally cleaned in the last 60 days.

Do you do ceramic coatings or paint correction?

Ceramic coatings, paint correction, interior restoration, tint, wraps, and semis or commercial work are quoted per job. Request a quote or text us photos of the vehicle.

Will I get a reminder?

Yes. We text you a reminder before your appointment.
